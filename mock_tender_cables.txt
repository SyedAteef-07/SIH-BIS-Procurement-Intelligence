GOVERNMENT OF INDIA
CENTRAL PUBLIC WORKS DEPARTMENT (CPWD)
NOTICE INVITING TENDER (NIT)

Tender Reference Number: CPWD/ELEC/2026/089
Date: 11-09-2026

1. NAME OF WORK: 
Supply, installation, testing, and commissioning of electrical wiring for the new administrative block.

2. TECHNICAL SPECIFICATIONS FOR CABLES:
The contractor shall supply PVC insulated cables for power and lighting installations. 
The cables must be rated for working voltages up to and including 1100V.
All wiring materials must meet the required conductor resistance and insulation resistance metrics. 
The cables must be sheathed and highly durable. 
A standard voltage test must be conducted prior to final commissioning.

3. COMPLIANCE & SAFETY:
All electrical wiring installations must strictly follow the standard code of practice. 
The supplier must ensure the products carry the mandatory BIS ISI marking as required by the relevant Quality Control Orders (QCO) for Scheme-I product certification.
